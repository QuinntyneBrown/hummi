# L1 - High-Level Requirements

Source of truth: the HTML mocks in `docs/mocks/` (cast, catalogue and copy as defined there; the visual direction is the Magic Patterns designs "Hummi home screen" and "Hummi navigation & overlays"). The
assumptions in `docs/mocks/README.md` ("Open questions and assumptions") are confirmed: listening
works without an account and saving needs one; Hummi is listener-only (no upload, creator or
moderation tooling); reports cover copyright, wrong licence and harmful content and are reviewed
within 2 working days; offline means cached pages and saved tracks; sign-in is email and password
only; the generating model is shown on the track page and the licence page where it is known. The
catalogue is made only of real tracks from Pixabay Music under the Pixabay Content License (ADR 0021).

## L1-001: Discovery
The system shall let listeners discover freely licensed music through a personal Home
page, a Browse directory of genres and moods, and a Genre page listing the tracks of one genre.

## L1-002: Search
The system shall let listeners search songs and artists from a search overlay that opens on top of any page.

## L1-003: Catalogue pages
The system shall present a page for each track, artist and album, showing its details, its licence,
its source and, where known, the open-weights model that generated it, plus a licence and credit page
explaining how each track may be reused.

## L1-004: Playback
The system shall let anyone, with or without an account, play tracks through a persistent player bar,
a full-screen Now playing view and an up-next queue drawer, controllable by keyboard shortcuts.

## L1-005: Library and playlists
The system shall let signed-in listeners like tracks, follow artists, and create, rename, delete,
fill and save playlists, and keep these in a library available on every device they sign in on.

## L1-006: Sharing
The system shall let listeners share a link to a track or playlist that anyone can open and listen
to without an account.

## L1-007: Accounts
The system shall let listeners create a free account with email and password, confirm their email,
sign in, reset a forgotten password and sign out, and shall prompt signed-out listeners to sign in
when they try to save.

## L1-008: Settings
The system shall let signed-in listeners manage their profile, playback, appearance and notification preferences.

## L1-009: Notification centre
The system shall keep a list of notifications about new releases from followed artists, playlist
shares, play milestones and product changes, with read and unread status.

## L1-010: Track reporting
The system shall let listeners report a track for copyright, licence or safety problems without the
artist learning who reported it.

## L1-011: System feedback
The system shall tell listeners what is happening at all times: loading, empty, error, partial and
offline states on every screen, toasts after actions, page-wide banners, and dedicated not found,
forbidden, server error and offline pages.

## L1-012: Accessible, themed and responsive interface
The system shall be operable by keyboard, offer light and dark themes, and adapt its layout to the
viewport, including a header whose navigation collapses on narrow viewports.

## L1-013: Navigation and menus
The system shall give signed-in and signed-out listeners a persistent header with the primary
navigation, search and an account menu, and a menu on every track for the actions that apply to it,
and shall let a listener turn on a kids mode that limits what they see to music made for kids.

## L1-014: A catalogue of real Pixabay music
The system shall build its catalogue only from real tracks of Pixabay Music: every track, artist,
genre, cover and licence it shows is taken from the track's Pixabay page, its audio is the real
Pixabay file, a cover is generated only when the track has none, nothing is invented to pad the
catalogue, and every screen works with no tracks, one track or a few.
