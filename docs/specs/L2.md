# L2 - Detailed Requirements

Every criterion traces to a mock in `docs/mocks/` (`pages/<screen>/<state>.html`,
`dialogs/<id>/<state>.html`, `notifications/<id>/<state>.html`). Quoted copy is the exact copy
of the mock. Loading and error behaviour shared by many screens is specified once in L2-039 and
L2-040; toast behaviour in L2-041; dialog behaviour in L2-047.

## L2-001: Home default content
**Traces to:** L1-001

Home (`/`) opens with a featured album billboard followed by shelves of tracks.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a listener opens Home, when it loads, then the billboard shows the kicker "New album · Carnival 2025", the title "Mas on the Avenue", the artist "Kaiso Collective", the blurb "Twelve road-ready tracks recorded live on the Avenue — brass, iron and a whole band of feathers.", the meta "12 tracks · 46 min · Free to stream and share", and the buttons "Play album" and "Add to library".
2. Given the billboard, when it renders, then its artwork carries an "Opens with" chip showing the cover and title of the album's lead track (e.g. "Feathers Up").
3. Given the billboard, when the listener activates "Play album", then the lead track plays, the button reads "Pause", and the player bar appears (L2-013); when they activate "Add to library", then the toast "Added Mas on the Avenue to your library" is shown (L2-041).
4. Given Home has loaded, when it renders below the billboard, then it shows the shelves "Recently added" (note "Fresh this week"), "Top 10 on Hummi this week" (note "Most played across the islands"), "Soca" (note "Road march contenders"), "Kids" (note "Sing-alongs and bedtime songs"), "Steelpan & Calypso", "Dancehall" and "Island chill" (note "For slow afternoons"), in that order, each with a "See all" link.
5. Given the "Top 10 on Hummi this week" shelf, when it renders, then it is a track list whose rows are numbered by rank from 1 to 10 (ADR 0005).
6. Given a shelf wider than the viewport, when the listener hovers it, then "Previous" and "Next" arrow buttons page it horizontally; given the "Recently added" shelf, then its cards do not carry the "New" badge, while cards in other shelves do when the track is new.
7. Given a card, when the listener hovers or focuses it, then a play button appears on its cover; given the card is the current track, then its title is coral and the play button stays visible, with an animated equaliser before the title only while it plays.
8. Given Home, when it renders, then the footer reads "Every track on Hummi is free to stream and free to share." and "hummi.fm — free music, made in the open."

## L2-002: Home empty, partial and offline states
**Traces to:** L1-001

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a listener with no listening history, when they open Home, then it shows "Your Home fills up as you listen" with "Play a few tracks and we’ll line up what to hear next. Pick a genre to begin.", a "Browse genres" button to Browse, and a "Start with a genre" shelf of six genre tiles.
2. Given the "Soca" shelf fails to load while the rest succeeds, when Home renders, then that shelf shows "Soca didn’t load" with "Everything else on this page still works. Try the shelf again." and a "Try again" button, and the billboard and the other shelves still render.
3. Given the device is offline, when Home renders, then a warning banner reads "You are offline. Showing what was saved on this device. Playing tracks you have not heard recently needs a connection." and only the billboard and the cached shelves are shown.
4. Given a shelf whose tracks loaded but are none (for example a genre with no tracks yet), when Home renders, then that shelf is not shown; given no album is published, then Home shows no billboard and starts with its first shelf (ADR 0021).
5. Given the catalogue has no tracks at all, when Home loads, then it shows "No music yet" with "Hummi’s catalogue is empty. Tracks appear here once they are imported from Pixabay Music." and a "Browse genres" button to Browse.

## L2-003: Browse genres and moods
**Traces to:** L1-001

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given the catalogue, when a listener opens Browse (`/browse`), then the heading is "Browse" and the subtitle shows the genre count and freely licensed track count (e.g. "10 genres · 4,812 freely licensed tracks", "10 genres · 1 freely licensed track", "10 genres · no tracks yet").
2. Given Browse, when it loads, then a "Moods" shelf shows genre chips and an "All genres" shelf shows a tile for each of the ten genres: Soca, Kids, Steelpan & Calypso, Dancehall, Island chill, Parang, Chutney, Reggae, Zouk, Rapso.
3. Given any genre tile or chip, when the listener activates it, then the Genre page for that genre opens.
4. Given Browse fails to load, when it renders, then the error block title is "Genres didn’t load". Browse has no empty state.

## L2-004: Genre page
**Traces to:** L1-001

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given the Soca genre, when a listener opens `/genres/soca` (or chooses "Soca" in the header), then the heading is "Soca" and the subtitle shows the track count and genre description (e.g. "1,204 tracks · road-march riddims, J’ouvert mornings and fete anthems").
2. Given the Genre page, when it loads, then it shows "Play all" and "Shuffle" buttons and the sort chips "Newest" (pressed by default) and "Most played", above a numbered track list.
3. Given the sort chips, when the listener selects "Most played", then that chip is pressed, "Newest" is not, and the list is ordered by play count.
4. Given "Play all", when activated, then the genre's tracks play in list order; given "Shuffle", when activated, then they play in random order.
5. Given a genre with no tracks, when the page loads, then it shows "No Soca tracks yet" with "New tracks arrive every day. Try a neighbouring genre while you wait." and a "Browse genres" button.

## L2-005: Track list rows
**Traces to:** L1-001

Track lists appear on Home, Genre, Search, Track, Artist, Album, Playlist and Queue.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a track list, when it renders, then each row shows its position number, cover, title linking to the track page, and "{artist} · {duration}" (e.g. "Kaiso Collective · 3:42").
2. Given the currently playing track is in the list, when it renders, then that row is marked current and shows an animated equaliser labelled "Playing" instead of its number.
3. Given a row, when it renders, then it has a like button labelled "Like {title}" (not pressed), or "Remove like from {title}" (pressed) if already liked, and a "More actions for {title}" button.
4. Given a title too long for the row (e.g. "The Quiet Between Two Lighthouses, Revisited (Extended Dream Mix)"), when it renders, then the title is truncated without breaking the row layout.

## L2-006: Search overlay and results
**Traces to:** L1-002

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given any page, when the listener activates the header search field ("Search songs and artists"), presses "/" with no text field focused, or chooses "More from {artist}" in a track menu, then the search overlay drops in from the top over the page and the query field takes focus.
2. Given the overlay, when it is open, then it shows a Search field with a "Clear" button (only once text is typed) and a "Close" button; given the listener activates "Close" or presses Escape, then it closes and the page beneath is unchanged.
3. Given the query "pan", when results return, then the overlay shows a "Top result" card (cover, title, "{artist} · {duration}"), an "Artists" list of up to 3 and a "Songs" list of up to 7, each song with cover, title, artist, duration and a "More actions for {title}" button on hover.
4. Given a song result, when it is the current track, then its title is coral and shows an equaliser while it plays.
5. Given a search in progress, when results have not returned, then skeleton rows are shown in place of the lists.

---

## L2-007: Search empty, no-results and error states
**Traces to:** L1-002

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given an empty query, when the overlay opens, then it shows a "Recent" list of earlier searches (e.g. "Kaiso Collective", "panorama", "lullaby", "Shanique"), each with a clock icon, and a "Browse by sound" grid of genre tiles, each with its title and "{n} tracks".
2. Given a recent search, when the listener selects it, then it fills the query and runs it again.
3. Given a genre tile, when the listener selects it, then the Genre page opens.
4. Given the query "rainfall in b-flat" with no matches, when results return, then the overlay shows "Nothing matches “rainfall in b-flat”" with "Try an artist name, or a sound like soca, pan or lullaby."
5. Given search is unreachable, when the query is run, then the overlay shows "Search isn’t working right now" with "We couldn’t reach search. Your query is still in the box; try again in a moment." and the query remains in the field.

**Traces to:** L1-003, L1-014

**Acceptance Criteria:**

Given a Pixabay track "{title}" by "{artist}", when a listener opens `/tracks/{slug}/licence`, then the heading is "Licence and credit" with "How you can reuse what you hear on Hummi" and an info alert "{title} is under the Pixabay Content License" with "{artist} shared it on Pixabay under the Pixabay Content License. ## L2-013: Player bar
**Traces to:** L1-004

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a track is loaded, when the listener is on any page, then a "Player" region at the bottom slides in and shows the cover, title and artist (opening Now playing), a like button, a "More actions" button, "Previous track", "Pause"/"Play" and "Next track" buttons, a "Track position" scrubber with elapsed and total time (e.g. "1:14" and "3:42"), and "Share", "Queue", "Volume" and "Expand" buttons.
2. Given nothing is loaded, when a page renders, then no player bar is shown, and it appears as soon as any track is played.
3. Given a track is playing, when the listener activates "Pause", then playback pauses and the button becomes "Play"; activating "Play" resumes it; a track that is playing shows an animated equaliser before its title.
4. Given "Next track" or "Previous track", when activated, then the next or previous queued track plays.
5. Given "Queue", when activated, then the queue drawer opens (L2-016); given its cover, title or "Expand", then Now playing opens (L2-015).
6. Given playback stops because the connection dropped, when the player renders, then its meta line reads "Playback stopped. Check your connection."

## L2-015: Now playing
**Traces to:** L1-004

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a track is loaded, when the listener opens Now playing from the player bar, then a full-screen layer fades in with a "Close" button, the line "Playing from {genre}", the large cover, the title, the artist, a full scrubber with elapsed and total time, and the controls "Shuffle", "Previous track", "Pause"/"Play", "Next track" and "Repeat".
2. Given "Shuffle" or "Repeat", when activated, then its pressed state toggles and playback order or repetition follows it.
3. Given Now playing, when it renders, then it shows "Save" (reading "Saved" once saved), "Share", "Queue" and "More actions" buttons; "Share" opens the Share dialog (L2-025), "Queue" the queue drawer (L2-016) and "More actions" the track menu (L2-053).
4. Given Now playing, when it renders, then an "Up next" list shows the next two queued tracks with cover, title, artist and duration.
5. Given Now playing is open, when the listener activates "Close" or presses Escape, then it closes and the player bar remains.
6. Given nothing is playing, when Now playing is opened directly, then it shows "Nothing is playing" with "Press play on any track and it shows up here with the waveform and queue." and a "Browse genres" button.
7. Given the connection drops while streaming, when Now playing renders, then a danger alert reads "Playback stopped" with "We lost the connection while streaming {title}. We’ll resume from {position} once you’re back online.", and playback resumes from that position when the connection returns.

## L2-016: Queue
**Traces to:** L1-004

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a queue, when the listener opens it from the player bar or Now playing, then a non-modal drawer opens at the top right of the page without a backdrop, headed "Queue" with a "Close" button, showing a "Now playing" card with the current track and an "Up next · {n}" list.
2. Given a non-empty "Up next" list, when the listener activates "Clear", then it is emptied; given a row, when the listener activates its remove button, then only that track is removed.
3. Given the queue, when it renders, then a "Then, more {genre}" list of up to four tracks follows, dimmed and showing durations.
4. Given an empty queue, when the drawer opens, then it reads "Use Play next or Add to queue on any track to line it up here."
5. Given a track's menu, when the listener chooses "Add to queue", then the toast "Added “{title}” to your queue" is shown; when they choose "Play next", then the toast "“{title}” will play next" is shown (L2-041), and the track is placed accordingly.
6. Given the drawer is open, when the listener presses Escape or activates "Close", then it closes.

## L2-017: Keyboard shortcuts
**Traces to:** L1-004

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given the page is focused and no text field is, when the listener presses Space, then playback toggles between play and pause; on a focused button or link, Space activates that control instead (L2-048).

5. Given the "Keyboard shortcuts" dialog, when it opens, then it reads "Work while the page is focused and no text field is." and lists the six shortcuts above with a "Close" button.

## L2-020: Add to playlist
**Traces to:** L1-005

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a signed-in listener with playlists, when they open "Add to playlist" for Feathers Up, then the dialog "Add “Feathers Up” to a playlist" reads "Pick one or more. You can change this later." and lists their playlists as checkboxes with track counts (playlists already holding the track checked), a "New playlist" link, "Cancel" and "Save".

7. Given the connection drops while adding, when it fails, then the danger toast "Couldn’t add the track" with "The connection dropped before we could save. Nothing was added." and a "Retry" action is shown.

## L2-027: Sign up
**Traces to:** L1-007

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a visitor, when they open Sign up (`/sign-up`), then it shows "Create your free account" with "Save playlists, follow artists and keep your place across devices.", the required fields "Display name", "Email" and "Password" (help "At least 10 characters"), the checkbox "I agree to the terms and the community rules" with "Hummi is free. You can listen without an account, but saving needs one.", a "Create account" button, and "Already have an account? Sign in".
2. ## L2-056: Import tracks from Pixabay Music
**Traces to:** L1-014

`hummi import` turns the Pixabay track pages listed in the manifest into manifest entries and cached audio (ADR 0021).

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a source URL of a Pixabay music track page, when `hummi import` runs, then the manifest gains one entry with the Pixabay id, the source URL, the title, the artist and their profile URL, the duration in seconds, the Pixabay genre and tags, the cover image URL (or none), the audio URL, file name, size and SHA-256, and the licence's name, URL and text, all read from the track's page and the licence page it links to; and the audio is saved to the media cache as `pixabay/{id}.mp3`.
2. Given a page that lacks a title, an artist, a duration, an audio file or the Pixabay Content License, when it is imported, then no entry is added, nothing is filled in, and the command reports the URL and what was missing.
3. Given a URL that is not a Pixabay music track page, or a page or file that cannot be fetched, when it is imported, then it is refused and reported and the other sources are still imported.
4. Given a track already in the manifest, when `hummi import` runs again, then its page is not fetched again; given its audio is missing from the media cache, then the audio is downloaded again and kept only if its SHA-256 matches the manifest.
5. Given the import ends, when it reports, then it prints how many tracks were imported, already present and refused, and the manifest is written with its tracks in source order.
6. Given a folder of pages saved from a normal browser (`{id}.html` for a track page, `license-summary.html` for the licence summary), when `hummi import --pages {folder}` runs, then the pages are read from that folder and only the audio is downloaded; a page with no saved file is refused as one that cannot be fetched, with a note on how to save it.
7. Given a track page as Pixabay publishes it, when it is read, then the title is the title the player shows, without the " by {artist}" and " | Royalty-free Music" endings Pixabay adds (a bar inside the title stays); the cover is the track's own image under `cdn.pixabay.com/audio/`, and none when the page has only a generic image such as a gradient; and the licence text is the licence summary alone, without the site's menus or cookie-consent panel.
