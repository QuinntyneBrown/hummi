Billboard

Overview

The first block of Home (L2-001). It names the page with its h1. Play album plays the lead track and reads Pause while the album plays; Add to library saves the album.

Anatomy

  1. KickerFor example “New album · Carnival 2025”
  2. TitleThe page's h1
  3. ArtistLinks to the artist
  4. Blurb and metaOne sentence, then “{n} tracks · {length} · Free to stream and share”
  5. ActionsPlay album and Add to library
  6. ArtworkThe cover with the “Opens with” chip naming the lead track

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A region named “Featured”; the title is the page's h1.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through the artist link and the actions

Focus

No focus on load.

Labelling

Blurb

Motion

No motion beyond colour transitions, which collapse to zero under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Targets are at least 24px and 44px on touch-primary controls.

Content

Keep the blurb to one sentence. The meta line always ends “Free to stream and share”.
