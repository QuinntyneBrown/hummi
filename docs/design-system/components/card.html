Overview

Use cards for self-contained groups such as settings sections. For lists of media, use the media tile instead.

Anatomy

  1. ContainerSurface with border, --radius-lg
  2. HeaderTitle, optional actions

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A card is a container. An interactive card has exactly one link or button covering it; do not nest controls.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the card’s link or button

Focus

Ring around the whole card when it is a link.

Labelling

The heading names the card; the link text is that heading.

Contrast

Text on card

Motion

No motion beyond colour transitions, which collapse to zero under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Targets are at least 24px and 44px on touch-primary controls.

Content

Card titles are nouns in sentence case.
