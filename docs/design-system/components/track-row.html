Track row

One track in a list: number, cover, title, artist, duration and actions.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

The workhorse of Hummi: used in playlists, search, queue and genre pages. The title links to the track page; actions stay on the row.

Anatomy

  1. Number or equaliserIndex; equaliser bars while playing
  2. Cover3rem, decorative
  3. Title and metaTruncate with ellipsis, never wrap
  4. ActionsLike and More, 44px targets

Responsive behaviour

Under 640px the artist and duration share one line under the title; the number column hides on screens narrower than 360px.

Theming

Component tokens alias semantic tokens only; re-theme by overriding the semantic tokens, never the component CSS.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

List item in an <ol>; the playing row has aria-current="true".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabTitle link, Like, More
EnterOpens the track page
Space on LikeToggles like

Focus

Ring on each control, not on the row.

Labelling

Like and More buttons name the track: “Like Glass Rain”.

Contrast

Title
Meta
Playing row

Motion

Equaliser bars are static: the glyph shows playing without animation.

Touch

Targets are at least 24px and 44px on touch-primary controls.

Content

Meta is “Artist · duration”. Long titles truncate; the full title is on the track page.
