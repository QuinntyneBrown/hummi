Toast pill

A short confirmation pill centred above the player bar, with the track cover and an optional Undo.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

ToastService.show() queues pills in hm-toast-region; each dismisses itself after 4 seconds. Problems are banners or inline errors, never toasts.

Anatomy

  1. LeadTrack cover, or the coral dot
  2. MessageOne bold line
  3. UndoOptional action that reverts

Sizes

Not applicable: Width follows the message, up to the viewport less 2rem.

States

Responsive behaviour

Centred horizontally; sits above the player bar, or 1.5rem from the bottom (toast-region--noplayer) when no player is shown.

Theming

Component tokens alias semantic tokens only; re-theme by overriding the semantic tokens, never the component CSS.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The region is role="status" with aria-live="polite".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches Undo

Focus

Toasts never take focus.

Labelling

The message is the content.

Contrast

Message

Motion

No motion beyond colour transitions, which collapse to zero under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Targets are at least 24px and 44px on touch-primary controls.

Content

Messages: “Added “{title}” to your queue”, ““{title}” will play next”, “Saved “{title}” to your library”, “Removed “{title}” from your library”.
