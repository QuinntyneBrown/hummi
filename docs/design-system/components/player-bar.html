Player bar

Overview

Shows the current track and the core controls. It links to the full Now playing page. Pages reserve room for it with has-player.

Anatomy

  1. Now playingCover, title and artist; links to Now playing
  2. ControlsPrevious, play/pause, next
  3. ScrubElapsed time, progress, duration
  4. Extras (desktop)Queue and volume

VariantDefaultHoverFocusDisabled

Responsive behaviour

Below 1024px the bar spans the full width on two rows (track and controls, then scrub). From 1024px it sits to the right of the sidebar in three columns.

Theming

Component tokens alias semantic tokens only; re-theme by overriding the semantic tokens, never the component CSS.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<section aria-label="Player"> with buttons; position uses a labelled progress bar.

Keyboard

KeyAction
SpacePlay or pause when focus is not in a text field
← / →Previous or next track
TabNow playing link, controls

Focus

Ring on each control; focus is never moved by track changes.

Labelling

Controls are named by action; the play button’s name flips between Play and Pause.

Contrast

Title
Meta
Time

Motion

No motion beyond colour transitions, which collapse to zero under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Controls are 44px; the scrub rail has a 24px-tall hit area.

Content

Title and artist truncate to one line. Playback errors replace the artist line: “Playback stopped. Check your connection.”
