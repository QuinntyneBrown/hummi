Account menu

The menu under the header avatar: who is signed in, the account destinations, then Sign out.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable
  • Overview
  • Anatomy
  • Variants
  • Sizes

Overview

Opens from the avatar button in the header, right-aligned below it. A transparent scrim closes it on outside clicks; the page beneath stays visible (L2-052). Destinations are added by the journeys that build them; Sign out is always last, below a divider.

Anatomy

  1. IdentityAvatar initial, display name and email
  2. ItemsProjected menu items, such as Your library and Settings
  3. Sign outBelow a divider; opens the Sign out of Hummi? dialog

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="menu" “Account” with menuitem items; the avatar button has aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded.

Content

Show the display name and the full email address. The last item reads “Sign out”.
