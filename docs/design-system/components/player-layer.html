Now playing layer

The full-screen layer for the current track: cover, scrubber, transport with Shuffle and Repeat, actions and Up next.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable
  • Overview
  • Anatomy
  • Variants

Overview

Opens from the player bar (cover, title or Expand) at /now-playing; the player bar hides while it is open. It renders the player state and emits every action; the host wires them (ADR 0009).

Anatomy

  1. TopClose pill and “Playing from {genre}”
  2. ArtLarge cover
  3. Title and artistDisplay title, muted artist
  4. Scrubberscrubber--full with elapsed and total
  5. ControlsShuffle, Previous, Play/Pause (hero), Next, Repeat
  6. ActionsSave, Share, Queue, More actions
  7. Up nextThe next two tracks

Responsive behaviour

Under 64rem the cover sits above the details in one column; from 64rem the cover (up to 32.5rem) and the details sit side by side.

Theming

Component tokens alias semantic tokens only; re-theme by overriding the semantic tokens, never the component CSS.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="dialog" with aria-modal and the label “Now playing”; Shuffle and Repeat use aria-pressed; Up next is a labelled region.

Keyboard

KeyAction
EscapeCloses the layer
TabClose, controls, actions
Space / → / ←Shortcuts (L2-017)

Focus

Close takes focus on open; focus returns to the page on close.

Labelling

The play button is named Pause or Play; More actions names the track.

Contrast

Title
Playing from
Hero icon

Motion

The layer rises in over 260 ms; reduced motion removes it.

Touch

Targets are at least 24px and 44px on touch-primary controls.

Content

Lost connection: “Playback stopped” with “We lost the connection while streaming {title}. We’ll resume from {position} once you’re back online.” Nothing loaded: “Nothing is playing” with Browse genres.
