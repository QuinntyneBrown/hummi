Chip

A compact toggle or link, used for filters and genres.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Use for filters (toggle) or genre shortcuts (link). Removable chips carry a labelled remove button.

Anatomy

  1. LabelOne or two words
  2. Pressed stateAccent subtle background

Sizes

Not applicable: One size, 32px tall.

States

VariantDefaultHoverFocusPressed

Both themes

Responsive behaviour

Rows of chips use pill-row to scroll horizontally on phones.

Theming

Component tokens alias semantic tokens only; re-theme by overriding the semantic tokens, never the component CSS.

Ambient
Ambient

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Filter chips are toggle buttons (aria-pressed); genre chips are links.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter / SpaceToggles a filter chip

Focus

Standard ring.

Labelling

Visible text names the filter.

Contrast

Pressed

Motion

No motion beyond colour transitions, which collapse to zero under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Targets are at least 24px and 44px on touch-primary controls.

Content

Sentence case, one or two words.
