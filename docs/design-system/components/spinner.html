Spinner

A small rotating ring for short, unknown waits inside a control.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Use inside a busy button or next to a short label. For page loads use a skeleton.

Anatomy

  1. RingBorder with a transparent quarter

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Wrap in role="status" with hidden text such as “Loading tracks”.

Keyboard

KeyAction
n/aNot interactive

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

Contrast

Ring

Motion

Rotates once per second; shows a static ring under reduced-motion.

Touch

Targets are at least 24px and 44px on touch-primary controls.

Content

Pair with a gerund: “Saving…”.
