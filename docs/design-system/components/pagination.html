Overview

Use for result lists that people may share or return to. For feeds, use “Load more”.

Anatomy

  2. Page linkNumber; current has aria-current

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<nav aria-label="Pagination"> with links.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves between links

Focus

Standard ring.

Labelling

Previous and Next have aria-label; disabled ends use aria-disabled.

Contrast

Current page

Motion

No motion beyond colour transitions, which collapse to zero under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Targets are at least 24px and 44px on touch-primary controls.

Content

Say “Showing 1–20 of 38”. Never “Page 1 of ?”.
