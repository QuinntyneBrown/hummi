States

Every state has copy: loading (“Loading tracks”), empty, error, success, busy (“Saving…”).

Responsive behaviour

Copy is the same at every width; truncation happens in CSS, not in the text.

Accessibility

  • Link text makes sense out of context.
  • Messages say what changed, not just a colour.

Content

Sentence case, verbs on buttons, no exclamation marks.
