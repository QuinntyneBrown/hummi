Notifications

Toast vs banner vs notification centre, priority and auto-dismiss rules.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Three channels with different lifetimes.

When to use what

ChannelLifetimeUse
Toast8 s; errors persistResult of an action
BannerUntil resolved or dismissedPage-wide state: offline, maintenance, verify email
Notification centreUntil readEvents from other people or the system

Structure

Added to queue
“Glass Rain” plays after the current track.
Scheduled maintenance
Read-only on Sunday 1 Nov, 02:00 to 02:30 UTC.

States

Stacked toasts show at most three; older ones collapse. Danger toasts never auto-dismiss. The unread count lives on the Notifications link and the bell.

Responsive behaviour

Toasts are full width on phones and bottom right from 640px; banners sit above the top bar.

Accessibility

  • One polite live region exists before any toast arrives.
  • Unread dots and counts have spoken labels.

Content

Past tense for events; specific actors and objects.
