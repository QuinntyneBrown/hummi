Foundation

Elevation

Four shadow levels and nine layers. Dark theme uses lighter surfaces and darker shadows to separate layers.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable
  • Overview
  • Shadow levels
  • Surface hierarchy
  • Z-order
  • Usage
  • Tokens

Overview

Elevation expresses what sits above what. Cards are level 1, raised cards and dropdowns level 2, menus and popovers level 3, dialogs and sheets level 4. In dark theme, raised surfaces are also lighter than the canvas.

LayerTokenContains

Toast--z-toastToasts
Tooltip--z-tooltipTooltips

Usage

Components never set a raw z-index; they use a layer token. Do not stack more than two shadow levels in one view.
