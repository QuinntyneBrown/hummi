Overview

Hummi sounds like a friendly record-shop regular: warm, brief and never salesy. We are honest that the music is AI-generated and openly licensed, and we say so plainly.

Voice and tone

SituationToneExample
SuccessQuiet, specificAdded to Late-night coding.
ErrorCalm, owningWe couldn’t save your changes. Try again.
EmptyEncouragingYour library is empty. Like a track and it lives here.
DestructiveDirectDelete “Late-night coding”? You can’t undo this.

Rules

  • Sentence case everywhere, including buttons and titles.
  • Buttons are verbs: Add to playlist, Create account.
  • Dates read “Tue 27 Oct 2026”; durations “4:12” and “1 h 32 min”; counts use thousands separators (128,904).
  • No exclamation marks, no “Oops”, no blame.
  • Say “track”, “playlist”, “artist”; say “generated with an open-weights model” rather than “AI magic”.

Error message formula

What happened + what is safe + what to do. “We couldn’t load this page. Your music hasn’t gone anywhere. Try again.”

Empty state formula

What is empty + why + one next step.
